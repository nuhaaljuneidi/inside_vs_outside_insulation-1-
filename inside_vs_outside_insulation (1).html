🎯 The Key Lesson

Same R-value ≠ Same Performance!

Location of insulation matters because of thermal mass placement

✓ Insulation OUTSIDE (Exterior Insulation):

  • Mass is inside thermal envelope
  • Mass helps stabilize indoor temperature
  • Reduces temperature swings
  • Lower peak loads
  • Better comfort
  • Lower energy bills

✗ Insulation INSIDE (Interior Insulation):

  • Mass is outside thermal envelope
  • Mass can't interact with room
  • Indoor temp fluctuates more
  • Higher peak loads
  • Poorer comfort
  • More energy consumption

This is why continuous exterior insulation is preferred for massive walls!
